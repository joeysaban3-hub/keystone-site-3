What We Do

Our Bathroom & Tile Services

From full custom remodels to waterproofed showers and heated tile floors — built to last and finished clean.

Full Bathroom Remodels

Complete top-to-bottom renovations — layout, plumbing, tile, fixtures and finishes done to a high standard.

Tile Installation

Floor-to-ceiling tile, large-format, mosaic and accent work installed flat, level and built to last.

Custom Showers

Basement Bathrooms

Add a full or half bath downstairs — framing, plumbing, waterproofing and tile handled start to finish.
